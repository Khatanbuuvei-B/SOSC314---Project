# SOSC314---Project

The Framing Narratives of the “Green Data Centers” Project in Mongolia

Background: UNCCD hosted its 17th Conference of the Parties (COP17) in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, last August under the theme Restoring Land, Restoring Hope. Its aim was to find solutions to desertification, land degradation, and drought by bringing together leaders, scientists, youth, indigenous people, pastoralists, and farmers, and facilitating various discussions and meetings among them (UNCCD 17th Session of the Conference of the Parties, n.d.). 

One of the main outcomes achieved from the conference in terms of the economy was the agreement to position Mongolia as a new global data center hub, with a capacity of 863 megawatts. This decision was achieved due to Mongolia’s advantages: cheap renewable energy, a naturally cold climate that will reduce cooling costs, vast land, and a stable legal environment (Brkic, 2026). The project will be held under the title “Green Data Center”.
