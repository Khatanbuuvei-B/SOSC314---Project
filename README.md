# SOSC314---Project

The Framing Narratives of the “Green Data Centers” Project in Mongolia

**Background:** UNCCD hosted its 17th Conference of the Parties (COP17) in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, last August under the theme Restoring Land, Restoring Hope. Its aim was to find solutions to desertification, land degradation, and drought by bringing together leaders, scientists, youth, indigenous people, pastoralists, and farmers, and facilitating various discussions and meetings among them (UNCCD 17th Session of the Conference of the Parties, n.d.). 

One of the main outcomes achieved from the conference in terms of the economy was the agreement to position Mongolia as a new global data center hub, with a capacity of 863 megawatts. This decision was achieved due to Mongolia’s advantages: cheap renewable energy, a naturally cold climate that will reduce cooling costs, vast land, and a stable legal environment (Brkic, 2026). The project will be held under the title “Green Data Center”.

However, the decision is met with public opposition. Some say this brings global attention, investments, and economic circles to Mongolia. However, others say this decision overlooks the detrimental environmental effects data centers result in. 

**Motivation:** Framing narratives from the news articles surrounding the topic will be studied because different news sources may selectively emphasise economic development, technological modernization, resource constraints, or environmental risks. Analyzing these frames reveals how the project is publicly presented and contested. Ideally, official news sources should present factual information with a neutral tone, allowing the public to form their own opinion. However, a selective dominance of a single framing may lead to negligence for other issues that exist but haven’t been equally presented. Thus, the aim of this project is to find whether different institutions construct the issue differently by comparing official and independent sources.

**Research Question:** Are there any dominant opportunity and risk frames in Mongolian online news coverage of the recently proposed Green Data Center initiative and how do the prevalence of frames differ between official government sources and independent news sources?
